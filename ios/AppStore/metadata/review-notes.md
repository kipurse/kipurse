Review notes (paste into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes):
Kipurse is a non-custodial spending wallet for AI assistants. The user's funds live in a smart-contract wallet that only the user controls
(passkey / Face ID). The app never holds keys for the user's money and does not sell or exchange cryptocurrency; adding money opens
Coinbase's licensed onramp. To review without any setup: open the app → "Try the demo" on the first screen (or Settings → Demo). The demo
shows balances, an AI payment notification, an approval with Face ID, a receipt, "Report a problem", pause and "Take everything back".
No login credentials are needed for the demo.
