Name: Kipurse
Subtitle: A safe wallet for your AI
Promotional text: Let ChatGPT, Claude and other AIs pay for things — only within the limits you set, with every payment on your lock screen.
Description:
Kipurse gives your AI assistants their own small spending wallet, so they can buy things for you without ever touching your bank card.
• You set the rules: per payment, per day, and in total. Your AI can't go over them — they're enforced by the wallet itself.
• New shop? You approve it with Face ID, right from the notification.
• See every payment as it happens, with a receipt, the shop's name and contact, and one-tap "Report a problem".
• Pause any AI instantly, or take all your money back in one tap.
• Add money with Apple Pay or a debit card (via Coinbase), or from Coinbase directly.
• Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Cursor, Muse and any MCP-compatible AI.
Kipurse is non-custodial: the money stays in a wallet only you control. Digital dollars (USDC) on Base.
